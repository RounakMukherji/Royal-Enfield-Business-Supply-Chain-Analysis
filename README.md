# Objective
The Project focuses on analyzing Royal Enfield business performance using a large-scale synthetic dataset of approximately 1.6M transactions.  The goal was to evaluate the business performance, revenue generation and inventory management issues while identifying the key operational challenges  in product and supply chain management.

# Tech Stack
- I have created the data using advanced Excel formulas. It contains 1.6M transactional data of 8Lakhs+ customers in 2025.
- Cleaned and structured the data in MySQL and performed logical analysis to get business findings with the help of advanced SQL concepts such as joins, window functions, aggregations at the database 127.0.0.1.
- Built interactive dashboards in Power Bi, developed DAX queries to visualize KPIs, trend analysis, and gaps in inventory to showcase business performance.

# Key Insights
- Company has achieved a MoM Growth of 3.70% on average with the highest growth 11.14% recorded in March.
- Profit Margin remains low i.e. 1.70% due to high manufacturing price 437.31bn in 2025 for all the orders indicating cost optimization opportunities.
- “Cruiser” is the top performing segment and “Hunter” be the most demanded model from Royal Enfield.
- Approximately 85.51% dealers have good stock of bikes whereas 9.64% face low stock issue.
- Physical Damage and Usual Servicing are the top 2 leading causes for bike after sale servicing.
- In Karnataka, the number of servicing reached on top with average rating 3.08. Needs service quality improvement

# SQL Analysis
Performed advanced SQL queries including 'Joins', 'Aggregation' or 'Window Function' to analyse business problems as
- top 3 most selling bikes in each engine segments
- most profitable bike in each segments
- high demand but low stock dealers
- MoM revenue growth percentage of the company
- running total profit

# Business Impact
- Cost Optimization opportunities are there to reduce manufacturing cost so that profit margin can be redeveloped.
- Auto stock alert can be implemented to avoid potential stock out as 9.64% dealers face low stock.
- Provided actionable insights to improve revenue and inventory planning

# Conclusion
The project demonstrates end-to-end data analysis from data generation to business insights using Excel, SQL and Power Bi.
